# Short-Form Video Survey Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes survey responses about short-form video consumption and its relationship with cognitive functions.
The survey was distributed randomly among residents of Riyadh, with 107 participants and 28 questions.

## Research Objectives
- Measure short-form video consumption patterns.
- Assess the general level of cognitive functions among participants.
- Examine the relationship between short-form video consumption and momentary forgetting.
- Investigate differences in cognitive endurance based on daily viewing time.

## Statistical Methods
- Descriptive Statistics
- Cronbach's Alpha
- Student's T-test
- Chi-Square Test
- Pearson Correlation
- Analysis of Variance (ANOVA)

## Main Findings
- The Cronbach's Alpha values for the two study dimensions were 0.872 and 0.849.
- A statistically significant positive correlation was found between short-form video consumption intensity and momentary forgetting (r = 0.199, p = 0.041).
- No statistically significant difference was found in cognitive endurance across daily viewing-time groups (F = 1.854, p = 0.142).
- The most commonly used platform among the participants was TikTok.
